What the data shows

  1. Class determined chances. 1st class passengers survived at roughly 48%, more than 3rd class passengers at 28% — try the class filter above to see the gap directly.
  2. Sex was the strongest single factor. Women survived at about 71%, compared to 15% for men.
  3. Younger passengers fared slightly better. Survivors were on average about 4 years younger than non-survivors.
  4. Fare tracked with class and survival. Higher-fare tickets cluster with both higher class and higher survival, visible in the fare-vs-age chart.
  5. Port of embarkation mattered too. Passengers who boarded at Cherbourg had a noticeably higher survival rate, likely linked to a higher share of 1st class tickets sold there.
